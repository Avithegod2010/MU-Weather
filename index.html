MU Weather · Material You

Everything a weather app should do

Built across 14 releases by one hobbyist developer and the open-source community — every feature works without an account or a key.

📅

16-day forecast

Hourly scroller, trend chart with an ensemble P10–P90 confidence band, and tap-to-expand day detail.

🌧️

Animated rain radar

Playback, scrubbing and pinch zoom on a pure-JS tile renderer — no Google Maps, no billing.

🚨

Official warnings

Government alerts from MeteoAlarm in Europe and the US National Weather Service, with opt-in rules for fog, black ice, storms and more.

🧩

Five widgets

2×2, sunrise countdown, 4×2, a 4×3 dashboard and a multi-city widget with its own config screen — all sky-themed.

🎨

Material You

The palette follows your wallpaper on Android 12+, with 10 extra themes, density and icon-style options.

🗣️

Spoken digest

A daily readout of your day — optionally upgraded to a natural-language summary by the AI digest (bring your own key; everything works without one).

🫁

Air quality & pollen

US/EU AQI scales, 24 h pollutant curves, a 5-day outlook and a 30-day history strip.

🎯

Accuracy tracking

MU Weather scores its own forecasts against reality and ranks weather models on a leaderboard.

📔

Weather journal

Rate your days and the app learns what to wear — plus record breakers, year in review and climate normals.

🔔

Alerts that respect you

Quiet hours, snooze/dismiss buttons, per-city cooldowns and a custom alert builder with notes.

Private by architecture, not by policy

Privacy claims are cheap. MU Weather's are structural — there is no server to trust.

No accountsInstall and use. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to delete later.
No trackingZero analytics, zero ads, zero crash reporters. Your location never leaves the device except as a coordinate in a weather API call.
No API keysEvery data source is a free public API — Open-Meteo, RainViewer, NOAA, MeteoAlarm. Nothing can be revoked, rate-limited by a vendor, or billed.
Open sourceMIT-licensed, every line reviewable. Verify the claims yourself.

16 languages, fully translated

Every catalog is a complete 935-key translation pass — including launcher shortcuts and the spoken voice — not a partial overlay.
