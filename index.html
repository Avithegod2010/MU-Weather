FAQ

Fair questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There is no catch. MU Weather is MIT-licensed open source with no ads, no accounts and no paid tier. It runs on free public APIs, so there's nothing to sell you and nothing to rate-limit behind a vendor's paywall.

Does it need an internet connection?

It's offline-friendly: forecasts, air quality and more are cached on-device with sensible TTLs, so the app stays useful on the subway or in the mountains — the radar and warnings, being inherently live, need a connection.

What about the "optional AI digest"?

If you paste your own Anthropic or OpenAI key in settings, the daily spoken digest is upgraded to a natural-language summary. Without a key — the default — nothing changes: the built-in template digest still reads your day aloud. Only the digest text is ever sent, never your location history.

Is there an iOS version?

Not yet. MU Weather is Android-first: Material You theming, widgets and launcher shortcuts are the heart of the experience. The codebase is React Native, so iOS isn't off the table eventually.

How accurate is the forecast?

The app measures itself: every forecast is scored against actual observations, you get a per-metric accuracy view and a model leaderboard (ECMWF, GFS, ICON, Météo-France, JMA…), and rain probabilities are locally calibrated from your own reports.

Can I trust the APKs?

Every release ships from reproducible Expo EAS builds with SHA-256 checksums published on the release page. The source of every release tag is public, so builds can be audited end-to-end.
